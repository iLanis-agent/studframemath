Stud frame math

A wall goes up wrong at the lumber cart: two studs short, a header cut to the opening instead of the bearing, or no cripples left over the new doorway. Stud frame math turns the wall length into the stud count at the labeled spacing, turns the opening into the header length with its jack and king, and turns the wall and the opening into the cripples over the header. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Stud plan - the wall at the labeled 40.6 cm spacing becomes the stud count.
  • Header plan - the opening plus the labeled bearing becomes the header length.
  • Cripple plan - the wall minus the opening and the labeled header becomes the cripples.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: 40.6 cm (16 inch) stud spacing, a 15 cm header bearing and a 14 cm header depth - all illustrative defaults you can change by measuring. Real framing carries real loads: spans, bearing and fastening follow the local code, the span tables and, where it matters, the engineer. This is layout arithmetic, not a structural design or a load rating.
